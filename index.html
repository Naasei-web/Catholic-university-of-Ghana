About the Catholic University of Ghana

The Catholic University of Ghana is a private university located in Fiapre, near Sunyani in the Bono Region. Inspired by the Catholic tradition of learning, the University is committed to academic excellence and the development of the whole person.

Through teaching, research and community engagement, CUG prepares students with the knowledge, practical skills and values to lead with integrity and contribute meaningfully to Ghana and the wider world.
